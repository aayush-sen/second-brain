name: os-reflect
description: Daily self-examination of the OS — improvement ideas, redundancies to eliminate, hygiene fixes — written as a morning briefing. Use for "reflect", "morning briefing", or the scheduled daily run.
---

Read `os/OS.md` and `os/VISION.md` first. Work only inside `os/`. This is the system looking at itself, once a day; the output is the briefing Aayush reads on the dashboard (localhost:8877) — it must answer "what needs me today?" in under two minutes of reading. Spec: `os/specs/2026-07-16-daily-reflection-product-spec.md`.

1. **Canvas first, then sweep.** Run `python3 os/scripts/canvas_sync.py refresh --wait 90 --trigger daily` before the sweep — it starts the dashboard if needed, queues a refresh for the Chrome extension, and waits a bounded 90s (result `success | login_required | failed | timeout`; never block longer, never ask for credentials). Then `python3 os/scripts/oslib.py reflect` emits the deterministic pre-read: WATERMARK (last briefing date), CANVAS (connection state, last attempt/success, urgent Canvas work, changed facts), HEALTH (incl. STALLED draft outreach), NEEDS, DUE, EPISODIC since the watermark, AUDIT (the claims to verify), open QUESTIONS, STATS. Open a record only when a finding warrants judgment. Read the newest briefing in `os/briefings/`: don't repeat its unactioned proposals verbatim — escalate, drop, or stay silent; once a proposal has a `questions.md` qid, cite the qid. Triage episodic `idea` lines into proposals or silence.
2. **Trust — verify the AUDIT list.** Every `ingested` line: grep the record it names and confirm the fold actually happened (headless agents misreport their own state; verify, never trust). Every `ingest-pending` line: fold it now. Both are audit catches under Fixes applied.
3. **Self-fix — the OS repairs itself daily (mandate 2026-07-28), propose only what he'd notice.** Read `os/memory/lint-latest.md` (written by the 5:45am `/brain-lint` routine; if missing or >36h old, note that in the briefing) and fold its judgment items into this step. Fix without asking: everything previously "mechanical" (cache rebuild via `python3 os/scripts/build_cache.py`, stale `updated:` corrections, dead cross-references, pruning briefings older than 14 days) PLUS implementation-level repairs — redundant or dead code in `os/scripts/` and `os/dashboard/`, a skill step that demonstrably fails its goal, two places doing one job. Hard limits: never change what the dashboard looks like or what it does (look and functions are frozen; implementation is free), never delete a record, never touch `raw/`, never alter this schema or CLAUDE.md. Any change in behavior Aayush would notice is a proposal, not a fix. Decisions he must make go to `os/questions.md` (`- [open] YYYY-MM-DD | id-slug | question`); `[answered]` lines are standing decisions — read them, never re-ask.
   **Gap hunt (2026-07-28).** While sweeping, actively ask: *what does the OS not know that would be genuinely helpful?* — an unknown deadline, a missing contact detail, an ambiguous goal, a record whose next step depends on a fact only Aayush has. Each real gap becomes a `questions.md` line. No quota in either direction: zero is valid, and the bar is "would his answer change what the OS does."
4. **Jobs — scan, then describe.** Run `/posting-scan` steps 1-3 (`python3 os/scripts/jobs.py scan`, then describe the top 8-10 new postings with `jobs.py annotate`, then chase any followed company showing "none open"). The scan is cheap and deterministic; the describing is the judgment. A `SOURCE-FAILED` line goes in the briefing — a silently stale feed is worse than a missing one. The full pipeline-record half of posting-scan (step 4) is weekly work, not daily: run it here only when the feed surfaces something for a firm already in the pipeline.
5. **Insights — two feeds.** (a) `python3 os/scripts/daily_insights.py` (~1-2 min; every active last30days source over a 24h window; fixed generic queries — no OS content enters a search). Its output ends with an **"AI voices (last 24h)"** section — actual posts BY the prolific AI voices and labs (Altman, Karpathy, LeCun, Hassabis, Ng, Jim Fan, Brockman, Musk, Paul Graham; @OpenAI @AnthropicAI @nvidia @xai), pulled directly before the engine run so rate limits can't drop them. Trust that section as ground truth on who posted; only claim a voice was quiet if it says so. If it reads VOICES-UNAVAILABLE, say the pull failed — never claim silence. **Date-check every story before citing it**: each evidence item carries a date, and anything older than ~36h (common with Hacker News, where points accumulate over days) is either dropped or explicitly dated in the briefing — never presented as today's news. On PULSE-UNAVAILABLE or X throttling, fall back to `os/memory/cache/daily-insights-latest.md`. (b) **Newsletters from Gmail — full inbox sweep (2026-07-31).** Not a fixed roster: one search over the whole inbox for the last 24h (`newer_than:1d`, no category exclusions — newsletters land in Promotions, Updates, and Primary alike) and treat every recurring publication that arrived as a newsletter, whatever the sender. Skip welcome/sign-in/account/receipt emails and job-board mass mail — issues only. Read each issue enough to pull its 1-2 sharpest stories, then collate across all issues: dedupe against the pulse feed and against other newsletters (a story covered twice gets one line, attributed to whoever had the sharpest take). Known senders as of 2026-07-31 include Morning Brew (crew@, brewmarkets@, founderbrew@), The Information, a16z speedrun, The Leverage, and AI Made Simple — but the sweep, not this list, decides what's in. If Gmail fails, WebFetch https://www.morningbrew.com/ for the Brew alone. If everything fails, 1-2 WebSearches; if nothing credible surfaces, write "quiet day" — never fabricate or pad.
6. **Inbox — academic/research only.** Inbox since the watermark (`-category:promotions -category:social`; first run `newer_than:7d`). Surface concrete signal — deadlines, assignment instructions, research direction, advisor/lab email, meeting times — and fold durable facts into the records they touch (course pages, project records, `uni/deadlines.md`). Allowlist = the librarian's: academic/career/research only; NEVER personal (family, health, friends, money). Skip job-board mass mail (Handshake) unless it is a genuine target firm/role. Note folds under Fixes applied.
7. **Briefing — write `os/briefings/YYYY-MM-DD.md`** (frontmatter `type: briefing`, `title: Briefing — YYYY-MM-DD`, `updated:`). Target 600-750 words total — the 2-3 minute read (raised from 450 on 2026-07-31: the briefing must work as a replacement for reading the newsletters, not a teaser for them). Sections in order, all honest, none padded:
   - **State of play** — ≤3 sentences: what moved, what's live today.
   - **Needs you today** — terse bullets; the dashboard shows detail. Canvas warnings are mandatory here, never omitted: `login_required` ("Log into Canvas again — Pierce never needs your credentials"), stale (>36h) or failed sync, overdue or missing Canvas work, and material Canvas changes (moved deadlines, new assignments) since the last briefing. Include at most 3 job lines: a posting scoring ≥80, or any posting from a followed company, first seen today. Everything else stays in the feed on the Career page — this section is for what needs a decision, not a listing.
   - **Daily insights** — the day's 4-6 most important stories, drawn from BOTH the pulse and the newsletter sweep, each a 2-3 sentence block: what happened with the concrete numbers and details, then why it matters. Substantive enough that Aayush never needs to open the source or the newsletter it came from; this section is the read, not the index. Markdown-link and attribute each (pulse vs named newsletter). A sharp community quote may stand in for a headline.
   - **Newsletters** — the remainder, brief: one line per story with sender attribution, ONLY for stories Daily insights did not already cover. Link each line's sender/title to the email itself: `[Sender (MM-DD)](https://mail.google.com/mail/u/1/#all/<threadId>)` (the u/1/ segment is required: the UCSD address is the browser's SECOND Google account; unpinned links open the wrong inbox, and the email-address-in-path form does not work — confirmed 2026-07-31), using the thread id from the sweep (keep the ids noted while sweeping). Same for a Daily insights block whose source is a newsletter: link the attribution to its thread. No duplication between the two sections; on a day when the insights absorb everything, this section is short or absent (never pad).
   - **From your inbox** — terse, dated; omit the section entirely on a quiet day.
   - **Improvement proposals** — max 3, each concrete: what + one-line why + rough effort. Zero is valid; never pad.
   - **Redundancies / prune candidates** — what to eliminate and why it is safe.
   - **Fixes applied** — what the OS fixed in ITSELF this run: self-repairs from step 3, brain-lint follow-ups, folds, audit catches. Aayush's own fixes and reworks don't belong here — this section is the system's daily self-maintenance receipt.
8. **Trail.** `python3 os/scripts/oslib.py log filed "daily reflection YYYY-MM-DD — <n> proposals, <n> fixes"`, then one commit: `os: daily reflection YYYY-MM-DD`.

Quality bar (memory/preferences.md): one sharp observation beats five filler bullets. If the system is genuinely fine, say so in three lines and stop.
